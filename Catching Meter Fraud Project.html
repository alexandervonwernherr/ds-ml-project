01 · The case
Some clients tamper with their meters
The problem
Tampered meters mean energy is used but never billed. Every fraud the utility misses is lost revenue.
The constraint
Inspections cost staff and time. The utility cannot send someone to every one of its clients.
Our output
A fraud probability for every client, ranked so inspectors visit the riskiest first.
Audience: revenue-protection and inspection teams
Decision: which clients to inspect next

AUC, in plain words
Pick one fraud client and one honest client at random. AUC is the chance that the model gives the fraud client the higher risk score.
0.5 coin flip
1.0 perfect ranking
It rewards putting risky clients first, which is exactly what an inspection list needs.

07 · Recommendations
From model to a weekly inspection list
The data product
  • A ranked list of clients by fraud risk, refreshed weekly
  • Inspectors start at the top and work down
  • At the chosen cut-off, about 1 in 5 visits finds fraud
Recommendations
  • Set the cut-off by inspection capacity and the cost of a missed fraud
  • Treat backwards readings and frequent tariff changes as red flags
  • Record inspection outcomes and feed them back as labels
Next steps
  • Score each feature group on its own (ablation)
  • Add data, such as past inspection results
  • Try other algorithms, such as XGBoost or CatBoost
  • Calibrate scores, since weighting distorts probabilities
